# Bug Off!

A top-down arcade game for Jame Gam Hack Club. Theme: "I'm just a little guy."

Play as a mosquito, fly around and bite people while dodging their swings! A single hit will squash you, so make every bite count!

**Play it here:** https://finity16.itch.io/bug-off

## Controls

- **Move:** WASD
- **Aim:** mouse
- **Bite:** left click

Each bite flashes a white arc in front of you and stuns you for a moment, so don't swing carelessly. Strike your enemies while they are recovering from their attack. Every hit scores points, and kills score more. Clear a wave and the next one arrives, a bit tougher each time, featuring three unique enemy behaviors. There's no end, so see how high you can score.

## Enemies

- **Humans:** chase you and swing in an arc.
- **Spinners:** attack in a full circle, twice in a row.
- **Smart humans:** circle around you at a distance and lunge in when you're vulnerable.

## Built with

- [Godot 4](https://godotengine.org/) (GDScript), exported to the web
- Art drawn in Piskel
